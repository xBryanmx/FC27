Finishing a big SBC with low losses

  1. Submit your untradeable and spare club cards first. They cost nothing and count toward your score.
  2. Buy only the gap, and buy 84s. An 84 is worth twice the points of an 83 at about the same price.
  3. Enter your submitted score above, so the calculator only prices what is left.
  4. For hundreds of cards, buy in batches over several days. Prices climb as you clear the cheap listings, so the real cost is higher than the calculator's floor.
  5. For pack SBCs, check the odds table to see how many runs it takes to hit an 85+, 86+, 87+ or promo card, and what those runs cost.
  6. Stop when the reward value drops below the coins still to spend.

Stay on the right side of EA

EA has no public API for Ultimate Team clubs, and tools that log in as you or automate the Web App can get an account banned or transfer-locked. This site never touches your account. You type your submitted score in yourself.

Avoid sites that offer "auto SBC" or ask for your EA login.
