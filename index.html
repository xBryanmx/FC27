Getting a POTM cheap

No trick makes a POTM free. The cost is the score target times your price per point, so the steps below are about shrinking that. The table uses the fodder prices you set, plus any score you've already submitted.

POTM SBCTargetLeft to doCost at floor pricesPlan for (+20%)Ends in
  1. Count what you already own. Every card in your club counts toward the score, including duplicates and untradeables, and high ratings are worth a lot: an 86 is 4,100 points, a 93 is 25,000 and a 98 is 90,000. Check your club for untradeable pulls and unwanted cards before you buy anything.
  2. Buy only the rating with the lowest coins per 1,000 points. The fodder table marks it in green. Right now 84s and 85s are almost tied at about 1,145 coins per 1,000 points, and 82s and 83s are much worse. Re-check it each day, because prices move.
  3. Pick the POTM by cost, not by card rating. Targets are set by hand. Olise (91) asks for 700,000 points, while Raphinha (89) asks for 1,300,000. The table above ranks them for you.
  4. Buy over several days. Progress saves, and prices climb as you clear the cheap listings. The totals here are a floor, and guides suggest planning for about 20% more at real market depth. Spreading purchases gives listings time to refill.
  5. Search by hand. Filter by exact rating, sort by lowest Buy Now, and take the cheapest. Bidding instead of Buy Now can save a little but is slow. Avoid sniping and auto-buy tools, because they break EA's rules and can get your account banned.
  6. Don't farm fodder through Re-Rolls. One player's 120 Gold Re-Rolls spent 150,000 points and got back about 111,000 in cards. Only run a Re-Roll if you would use its cards anyway.
  7. Submit carefully. Pull cards off the transfer list first, because listed cards can't be submitted. Sort the submission screen deliberately and watch the running score, so you don't feed in the wrong cards by accident.
